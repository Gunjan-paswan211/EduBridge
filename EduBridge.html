Why EduBridge?

The problem

Students often struggle with scattered study resources, poor study planning and no clear view of their progress.

The solution

EduBridge combines learning resources, quizzes, a study planner and progress tracking into one simple platform.

The impact

Students study in an organized way, spot their weak areas early and steadily improve their learning outcomes.

    Built for students, by students

    EduBridge started as a hackathon idea: what if organizing your studies, practicing what you learn, and seeing your own progress lived in one clean place — instead of five different apps and a stack of notebooks?

    No backend, no sign-up walls. Everything you do here is saved right in your browser, so you can jump back in exactly where you left off.

    • Organize study material by subject
    • Practice with instant-feedback quizzes
    • Track streaks, scores and weak spots
    • Plan sessions with a lightweight planner
